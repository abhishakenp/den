# Tips & hidden gems

Everything in den that you won't find by looking at it. One line each, with a link to the full story.

## Sidebar & tabs

- **Click a pinned tab's favicon** when a **/** shows before its title to jump back to its pinned page. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#pinned-tabs)
- **Double-click a tab** to rename it. Clear the name and press Return to get the page title back. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#renaming)
- **Middle-click a tab** in the sidebar to close it. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#opening-links)
- **⌃Z** undoes the last sidebar action, up to 30 of them: a closed tab, a cleared Today, a deleted folder. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#undo)
- **⇧⌘T** brings back the last closed tab, or the Peek you just closed. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#the-library)
- **⌃Tab** jumps to the tab you used last, even in another space. **⌃⇧Tab** goes the other way. [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md#tabs)
- **⌘9** always goes to the last tab; ⌘1…8 count favorites first. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#switching-tabs)
- **⌘W on a pinned tab or favorite** only unloads it: it stays in the sidebar. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#the-three-kinds-of-tabs)
- **Drop a tab on the middle of a folder** to put it inside; near the edge it goes beside it. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#folders)
- **Drop a tab on a space icon** in the footer to move it to that space. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#drag-and-drop)
- **Drop a tab on the page** to split: the left third puts it on the left, the rest on the right. [Split view](peek-split-little-arc.md#split-view)
- **Drop a tab on the middle of another tab's row** to split the two. [Split view](peek-split-little-arc.md#split-view)
- **Drop a tab on a split's sidebar row** to add it as another pane. [Split view](peek-split-little-arc.md#split-view)
- **Click the speaker** on a tab playing audio to mute it. [Media](media.md#tab-audio-and-mute)
- **Drop links or files from other apps** onto the sidebar to open them as tabs. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#drag-and-drop)
- **Move the pointer to the left edge** when the sidebar is hidden (⌘S) to slide it back. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#hiding-the-sidebar)
- **Double-click the sidebar's edge** to reset its width. Drag it very narrow to hide it. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#hiding-the-sidebar)
- **Click the URL pill** to edit the address, same as ⌘L. [Command bar](command-bar.md#opening-it)
- **Hover the URL pill** for your pinned extensions and the puzzle menu. [Extensions](extensions.md#using-them)

## Spaces & themes

- **Swipe with two fingers in the sidebar** to switch spaces. [Spaces](spaces-and-themes.md#switching)
- **Double-click a space's title** to rename it. [Spaces](spaces-and-themes.md#the-space-menu)
- **Right-click a space icon or title** for its menu: icon, theme, profile, duplicate, move, delete. [Spaces](spaces-and-themes.md#the-space-menu)
- **Drag a space icon sideways** in the footer to reorder spaces. [Spaces](spaces-and-themes.md#creating-and-arranging)
- **Hover a space's title** for a **…** button that opens the theme picker. [Themes](spaces-and-themes.md#themes)
- **Esc in the theme picker** throws the change away; clicking outside keeps it. [Themes](spaces-and-themes.md#themes)
- **Type or paste any emoji** in the icon picker to use it as the space icon. [Spaces](spaces-and-themes.md#the-space-menu)
- **Profile ▸ New Profile** in the space menu gives a space its own logins and cookies. [Spaces](spaces-and-themes.md#profiles-separate-logins-per-space)
- **Drop a `.json` or `.toml` file in `~/.den/themes`** and it's in the command bar as **Theme: \<name\>**. [~/.den](den-home.md#themes)

## Links, Peek & split

- **⇧-click or ⌥-click any link** to open it in a Peek. [Peek](peek-split-little-arc.md#peek)
- **⌘Z within 15 s** of closing a Peek brings it back. [Peek](peek-split-little-arc.md#peek)
- **⌘O** turns a Peek or a Little Arc window into a real tab. [Peek](peek-split-little-arc.md#peek)
- **⌘-click** a link for a background tab; **⌘⇧-click** to open it in front. [Sidebar](sidebar-and-tabs.md#opening-links)
- **⌃⇧1…4** focus split panes; **⌃⇧-** closes the focused one; **⌃⇧=** adds one. [Split view](peek-split-little-arc.md#split-view)
- **Hover a split pane** for its close and "separate" buttons. [Split view](peek-split-little-arc.md#split-view)
- **Right-click a split's row** to switch between side by side, top and bottom, and grid. [Split view](peek-split-little-arc.md#split-view)

## Hover previews

- **Rest on a tab** for a card with what's in it and buttons for pin, split, duplicate, copy, mute, move and archive; after that, other tabs' cards show instantly. [Hover previews](hover-previews.md)
- **Press a card button's shortcut while the card is open** (⌘D, ⇧⌘C, ⌘W, ⌃⇧=) and it acts on the tab you're pointing at, not the selected one. [Hover previews](hover-previews.md#buttons-on-the-card)
- **Hold ⇧ over any link** on a page for a preview of where it goes: picture, title, summary, and Open in Peek / Split / Copy Link. [Hover previews](hover-previews.md#link-previews-hold--over-a-link)
- **Hover a GitHub PR tab** (or ⇧-hover a PR link) for its checks, diff size and conflicts, no sign-in needed. [Hover previews](hover-previews.md#the-github-pr-peek)
- **Hover a Calendar tab** for a **Join** button for your next meeting. [Hover previews](hover-previews.md#what-the-cards-show)
- **Hover a folder** to see its tabs without opening it. [Hover previews](hover-previews.md#what-the-cards-show)

## Command bar

- **Type a setting's name** and press Return to flip it, right there. [Command bar](command-bar.md#settings-from-the-bar)
- **Tab after a site keyword** (`yt`, `gh`, `w`, `maps`, `x`, `g`) searches that site. [Command bar](command-bar.md#site-search-keywords)
- **Tab** otherwise narrows the list to actions only. [Command bar](command-bar.md#keys-inside-the-bar)
- **⇧Return** opens a result in a Peek instead of a tab. [Command bar](command-bar.md#keys-inside-the-bar)
- **Type "Keyboard Shortcuts"** for every shortcut, searchable and runnable. [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md)
- **Press ⌘T again** to close the bar. [Command bar](command-bar.md#opening-it)
- **Type a tab's name** to switch to it in any space. [Command bar](command-bar.md#typing)

## Pages

- **⌥⇧⌘C** copies the page as a Markdown link. [Page tools](page-tools.md#copy-the-link)
- **⌘E** searches the page for the selected text. [Page tools](page-tools.md#find)
- **Zoom sticks per site**; ⌘0 forgets it. [Page tools](page-tools.md#zoom-remembered-per-site)
- **⌃⌘R** toggles Reader; **⇧⌘2** captures a region of the page. [Page tools](page-tools.md#reader)
- **Leave a playing video's tab** and it follows you in den's mini player. [Media](media.md#the-mini-player)
- **Type "dark"** in the command bar to force a site dark or light. [Privacy](privacy-and-passwords.md#dark-mode-for-every-website)

## Settings & config

- **⇧⌘B** opens the daily briefing, and you can change that key in Settings ▸ Briefing. [Briefing](connections-and-briefing.md#the-briefing)
- **"Always quit"** in the quit dialog turns it off for good; the **Ask Before Quitting** command turns it back on. [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md#app)
- **Any shortcut can be remapped** in `~/.den/config.toml`, and it applies on save. [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md#change-any-shortcut)
- **`[plugins] disabled = ["peek"]`** turns off any built-in feature, live. [~/.den](den-home.md#configtoml)
- **Drop Swift files in `~/.den/plugins/<id>/`** and den compiles and loads them as you save. [~/.den](den-home.md#plugins)
